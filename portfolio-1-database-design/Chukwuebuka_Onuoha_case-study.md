# Case Study: ECEWS SPEED Osun State Integrated HIV/TB Data Hub

## 1. Overview & Program Scope

The ECEWS SPEED Osun State Integrated Public Health Database manages decentralized client records across multiple Local Government Areas (LGAs) and supported health facilities in Osun State. The system handles epidemic control indicators, tracking the full clinical cascade from community-based HIV Testing Services (HTS) to Antiretroviral Therapy (ART) enrollment, client retention, TB screening/co-infection management, and laboratory viral load suppression monitoring.

## 2. Database Users

- **M&E Officers / Data Clerks:** Input facility-level service statistics, cohort tracking data, and high-frequency reporting indicators.
- **Clinicians / Case Managers:** Record ART enrollments, clinical staging, TB co-infection statuses, and treatment outcomes.
- **Community Outreach Workers:** Log community-based HTS testing sessions and client linkage coordinates.
- **Laboratory Technicians:** Input diagnostic results, primarily plasma viral load assay outcomes.
- **Program Coordinators:** Query LGA-level performance dashboards, retention rates, and treatment cascade indicators.

## 3. Major Data Stored

- **LGAs & Facilities:** Administrative boundaries (Osun LGAs) and supported healthcare delivery sites.
- **Clients:** Unique client identifiers (PEPFAR/National ID standards), demographic data, and geographical linkage.
- **Community Outreaches:** Mobile testing campaigns, screening logs, and HTS yield data.
- **ART Enrollments & Retention:** Initial clinical staging, regimen lines, multi-month dispensing (MMD) schedules, and active retention tracking.
- **TB Screening & Treatment:** Presumptive TB screening logs, GeneXpert results, and TB treatment co-infection outcomes.
- **Laboratory Records:** Longitudinal viral load test results and suppression classifications (<1000 copies/mL).

## 4. Key Operations Supported

- Registering clients from community outreaches and facility entry points.
- Enrolling verified HIV-positive clients into ART treatment cohorts.
- Tracking client retention on ART across scheduled appointments and identifying missed visits/defaulters.
- Screening active ART clients for TB symptoms, tracking diagnostic tests, and managing co-infection treatment cascades.
- Recording and analyzing routine laboratory viral load testing outcomes and suppression rates.
